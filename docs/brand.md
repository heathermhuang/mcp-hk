# mcp.hk logo

The linked H joins two geometric halves across an open diagonal seam. It represents
Hong Kong and the connection between public data and the applications that use it.
The lowercase wordmark spells out the full domain, including `.hk`.

- Master lockup: `src/mcp_open_data_hk/web/logo.svg` (176 × 40 view box).
- Small mark: `src/mcp_open_data_hk/web/favicon.svg` (32 × 32 view box).
- Both assets use vector paths and require no fonts, scripts or external resources.
- The transparent lockup uses the site's forest green and ink palette, switching
  to mint and pale ink when the viewer prefers dark mode.
- The favicon uses mint on a forest tile for contrast in either browser theme.
- Keep the lockup at least 132 px wide, preserve its proportions, and leave clear
  space of at least the mark's 6-unit stem width around the artwork.
- Use the square mark alone for favicons and small app icons; keep the full name
  visible in page headers and other places where people identify the service.

Source citations, agency names and provider logos are separate from this identity.
The logo does not imply government ownership or endorsement.
