# Design context

The creator wants mcp.hk to make Hong Kong public data understandable and useful
to people, application builders and AI clients. Repeated feedback identifies
confusing pages, inconsistent discovery and hard-to-find connected providers as
the problem. A successful first visit finds an available capability, understands
what it covers, and reaches real source-backed records without knowing API/file
internals. Developers also need exact HTTP/MCP examples, exposed progressively.

Use a direct, calm, source-aware voice in English and Hong Kong Traditional
Chinese. Keep the existing dark/mint visual system and Public Sans mcp.hk wordmark.
The creator explicitly rejected the ambiguous H/hk logo. Keep examples out of
primary navigation; include their underlying data in the same discovery system.

Priorities: task and capability first, implementation details second; truthful
coverage/freshness and source provenance; visible next actions and useful empty
states; durable URLs and safe selection state. Do not equate harvested metadata,
connected interfaces, successful record reads and complete government coverage.
